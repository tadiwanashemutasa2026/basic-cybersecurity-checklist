Use this checklist when you receive a suspicious email or message.

Check the sender's address.

Look for suspicious links and unexpected attachments.

Do not click links or open attachments.

Do not reply or provide personal information.

Report the message to your IT or cybersecurity team using the approved reporting method.

Follow your organisation's instructions for deleting or handling the message.

If you entered your password, contact the IT team immediately and change it through the official website.

Record any relevant details, such as the time received and the sender.

Important

Do not forward suspicious messages to other people unless your organisation's reporting procedure requires it. Never upload real passwords, patient records or other confidential information to a public repository.
